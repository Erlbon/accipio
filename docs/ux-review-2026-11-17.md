# Accipio: review of the menus and the UX, 2026-11-17 (build 2026-11-17#12, working tree)

You asked: "The settings is a bit crowded. can we do a critical review of the menu and ux of accipio?" This is that review. Nothing in the program was changed. Each card says what is wrong, shows the real window, and offers a fix.

**How it was checked.** Every window and menu was built from the real code and painted to a picture without being shown (normal Qt platform, `grab()`), in dark and in light, from sample downloads, a scratch settings folder and a stand-in for the keyring. No real profile, keyring, network or torrent program was touched. Each picture has a button to flip between dark and light. Menu texts, shortcuts and on/off states were read from the live menus, not from the code. The render script is `C:\Temp\Claude\accipio-ux-review\render.py`.

**Not checked.** Nothing was clicked by hand, so hover and focus states, tab order, the progress boxes, the archive box, the playlist question and the Windows notices are outside this review. Sizes on a small screen (card 6) are worked out from the windows' own sizes, not tried on such a screen. "Other download managers have it" is from knowledge, not a fresh look.

**Two kinds of card.** Cards 1 to 22 are a fix: Yes, No or Later. Cards 111 to 115 are real choices with lettered options and a small mockup in dark and light; "Keep as is" is always there. The numbers never change.

**Effort:** small = under 2 minutes of work, medium = up to 10, large = more than 10.

**Overall.** You are right about Settings: it is one flat form of 15 rows with no grouping at all, 28 things to operate, and it has reached 746 px in height (844 px when ffmpeg is missing and qBittorrent is picked). The rows fall into seven topics, so splitting it is easy (card 111). The menus are small and mostly sound, but they have three real faults: a switched-off menu line looks exactly like a working one, the Downloads menu is live with nothing selected, and the program calls the same window both Settings and Preferences. The main window's worst fault is that the Status column starts off-screen, so "Paused" and the reason for a failure are not visible in a new installation. Rules and Add links hold up well; their findings are small. Other Accipios shows both roles to everyone and sits three clicks deep.

## 1. Settings

### 111. How to split Settings
- **What:** `accipio/gui/settings_dialog.py:122-225` is one form: 15 rows, no headings, no tabs. In it are 19 buttons, ticks and radio buttons, 5 drop-downs and 4 number boxes (30 controls when ffmpeg is missing and qBittorrent is picked). The rows stand in the order they were built over three weeks, not in the order of use: the ffmpeg path comes before what happens to finished downloads. Three of the rows are not settings at all (cards 2 and 112).
- **What belongs together:** seven topics. **Downloads:** downloads at once, connections per file, total speed limit, finished downloads. **Video sites:** quality, ffmpeg, playlists. **Browser:** the browser's own files, and what the browsers are set to do. **Torrents:** the choice and qBittorrent's path. **Notifications:** the three notice lines. **Other Accipios** (see 112). **Program:** updates, debug log.
- **A:** tabs across the top, as the Rules window has. Familiar, but seven tabs fill the width already; an eighth topic would not fit.
- **B:** a list of pages on the left, the page on the right. Takes a new topic without a redesign, and long names ("Other Accipios", "Notifications") fit.
- **C:** one page as now, but with a heading over each topic and a scroll bar. Least work, everything still on one long page.

### 112. Where Other Accipios lives
- **What:** the link is set up in a window that opens from a "Set up…" button on row 12 of Settings (`settings_dialog.py:237-251`): Tools > Preferences > Set up…, three steps. No menu names it. That window saves at once and has only Close, while the Settings window under it has OK and Cancel, so Cancel in Settings does not undo what was done there.
- **A:** a page of its own inside the new Settings (card 111), with a line at its top saying that changes there apply at once.
- **B:** its own window under Tools > Other Accipios…, beside Rules… and Connect Browser…, which are the same kind of set-up. Settings loses the row.

[[mock:112]]

**Effort:** small. **Recommendation:** B (a window that applies at once does not belong inside one with OK and Cancel, and Tools is where the other set-up windows are).

### 113. One name: Settings or Preferences
- **What:** the menu says "Preferences…" (Tools, Ctrl+,). The button in the main window says "Settings…". The window's title is "Settings". The program's own texts use both: "Switch it on under Tools > Preferences" (`main_window.py:819`), "Settings > Other Accipios", "Settings saved."
- **A:** Settings everywhere, the menu too. It is the word Windows uses and four of the five places already say it. Accipio's menu then differs from the Redactor family's "Preferences…".
- **B:** Preferences everywhere: the button, the window title and the messages change. The family's menus stay alike.

[[shot:menus|The six menus. Tools says Preferences; the button beside the paste bar says Settings]]

**Effort:** small. **Recommendation:** A (the word people look for on Windows; fewer texts change).

### 1. The note about Clear Finished points at the wrong menu
- **What:** Settings says "Edit > Clear Finished works with every choice" (`settings_dialog.py:342`). The command is in File (`main_window.py:447`).
- **Why:** File is also the wrong home: every other command that acts on the list is in Downloads.
- **Fix:** move Clear Finished to the Downloads menu, under Remove from List, and correct the note.
- **Effort:** small. **Recommendation:** Yes.

### 2. Three rows in Settings are signposts, not settings
- **What:** "Downloads go to" shows the folder greyed out and says to change it under Rules > Places. "Archives" says only "Under Rules > 5 · Archives". The line about connections per file needing a restart is at the very bottom, eleven rows from its field.
- **Fix:** make the two signposts links that open Rules on the right tab, and put the restart note under its own field.
- **Effort:** small. **Recommendation:** Yes.

### 3. Number boxes are as wide as the window
- **What:** "3", "8" and "No limit" each sit in a box about 540 px wide, and so do the two drop-downs (picture under 111). The eye has to travel from the label to the little arrows at the far right.
- **Fix:** boxes as wide as their content, left-aligned, as the Archives tab already does (`archives_tab.py:31`).
- **Effort:** small. **Recommendation:** Yes.

### 4. What a choice means is hidden in tooltips
- **What:** the three torrent choices are explained only when the mouse rests on the drop-down (`settings_dialog.py:271-276`). The explanation of "Browser's own files" is a tooltip of about 90 words (`:169-174`). Three other notes are always on show. There is no way to know which rows have a tooltip.
- **Fix:** once Settings has pages (111) there is room: one plain sentence under each control, and tooltips only for extra detail.
- **Effort:** medium. **Recommendation:** Yes (together with 111).

### 5. Three labels do not name their setting
- **What:** "Video sites" is the label of a quality choice ("Best"). "Finished" is about when finished downloads leave the list. "Notify when" heads three ticks that each also pick a sound.
- **Fix:** "Video quality", "Finished downloads", "Notices and sounds".
- **Effort:** small. **Recommendation:** Yes.

### 6. Settings and Other Accipios outgrow a small screen
- **What:** Settings is 746 px tall, 844 px with everything showing, and has no scroll bar. Other Accipios asks for 887 px. A 1366 × 768 laptop, or a 1920 × 1080 one at 150 % scaling, has about 720 px. This is worked out from the windows' own sizes, not tried on such a screen: there the OK button would be below the edge.
- **Fix:** 111 solves Settings (each page is short). Other Accipios: let its two tables shrink and scroll.
- **Effort:** small. **Recommendation:** Yes.

### 7. Cancel in Settings does not undo everything
- **What:** three things in Settings happen at once and stay after Cancel: Install ffmpeg (`main_window.py:878`), Delete the log, and everything done in Other Accipios. Nothing on the buttons says so.
- **Fix:** 112 takes Other Accipios out. Delete the log asks first ("Delete debug.log now?"). The ffmpeg line says "installed" once it is, so it is clear that it is done.
- **Effort:** small. **Recommendation:** Yes.

## 2. Menus

### 8. A switched-off menu line looks like a working one
- **What:** in the right-click menu on a running download, Resume, Retry and Open are switched off, but they are drawn in exactly the same colour as Pause (measured in the picture: the same brightness). The stylesheet styles menu lines and has no look for a switched-off one (`accipio/gui/style.py:66-69`).
- **Why:** you click Resume, nothing happens, and nothing told you it could not.

[[shot:context|The right-click menu on three kinds of row. Resume and Retry are off on the first, Pause and Open on the others: no difference to see]]

- **Fix:** grey text for a switched-off line, in dark and light, and no highlight when the mouse is over it.
- **Effort:** small. **Recommendation:** Yes.

### 9. The Downloads menu is live with nothing selected
- **What:** with no row selected, all ten lines of the Downloads menu are switched on (read from the live menu). Each one then does nothing, silently. Only the right-click menu switches lines off by what is selected, and it switches them all back on afterwards (`main_window.py:785-800`).
- **Fix:** one function decides which lines fit the selection; the menu bar and the right-click menu both use it, whenever the selection changes.
- **Effort:** medium. **Recommendation:** Yes.

### 10. The right-click menu lacks "Open Link in Browser"
- **What:** the Downloads menu has it, the right-click menu does not (`main_window.py:785-787`). It exists for the failed download whose page wants a captcha, which is exactly the row you right-click. "Open" is also the only line in the menus without an underlined letter.
- **Fix:** add it to the right-click menu after Copy Link; "&Open".
- **Effort:** small. **Recommendation:** Yes.

### 11. No Pause All and Resume All
- **What:** pausing everything means Ctrl+A, then Space. The tray icon's menu has only Show Accipio and Exit. Free Download Manager, JDownloader and qBittorrent all have "pause all" in the menu and in the tray.
- **Fix:** Downloads > Pause All and Resume All, and the same two lines in the tray menu.
- **Effort:** medium. **Recommendation:** Yes.

### 12. Exit shows no shortcut
- **What:** File > Exit has no key beside it (read from the live menu). The code asks for the system's "quit" key, which Windows does not have (`main_window.py:449`); the code's own note at `:1008` says Ctrl+Q.
- **Fix:** give Exit Ctrl+Q outright.
- **Effort:** small. **Recommendation:** Yes.

### 13. Columns cannot be hidden
- **What:** the list has eight columns and View has only the four filters, which repeat the chips. There is no way to drop Speed or Rule if you never read them.
- **Fix:** View > Columns with a tick per column (Name always on), the same list on a right-click of the header, remembered with the widths.
- **Effort:** medium. **Recommendation:** Later (114 first; it may be enough).

## 3. The main window

### 114. The Status column starts off-screen
- **What:** in a new installation the window is 1000 px wide and the columns need more (`main_window.py:211`: Name 300, Progress 120, Folder 180, the rest 100 each). The list opens with a sideways scroll bar, even when empty, and Status, the last column, is out of sight. Status is where "Paused", "Waiting for Collector" and the reason for a failure are written.
- **A:** Status moves to second place, after Name, and the starting widths are set so that all eight columns fit in 1000 px. A window whose widths you have already changed keeps them.
- **B:** no Status column: the status is a second, smaller line under each name. Rows become taller; seven columns fit easily.

[[shot:main|The main window at its starting size. Lecture 12.mp4 is paused and Broken.zip failed with "404 Not Found": neither is readable without scrolling right]]

[[mock:114]]

**Effort:** medium. **Recommendation:** A (keeps the rows compact and every header sortable).

### 14. A paused download looks like a running one
- **What:** the bar of a paused download is the same blue as a running one (`queue_model.py:229-232`): in the picture above, Lecture 12.mp4 is paused and Ubuntu is running.
- **Fix:** a paused bar in grey, so blue always means "moving now".
- **Effort:** small. **Recommendation:** Yes.

### 15. The empty list says nothing
- **What:** a new installation shows a blank list. The only help is the grey text in the paste bar.

[[shot:main-empty|First start: the banner, an empty list, a scroll bar]]

- **Fix:** a line in the middle of the empty list: "Nothing here yet. Paste a link above, drop one on the window, or click a download in your browser."
- **Effort:** small. **Recommendation:** Yes.

### 16. The browser banner is dark brown in light mode too
- **What:** its colours are fixed (`browser_help.py:124`, `#5c4a14`), so in light mode it is the darkest, heaviest thing in the window (flip the picture above to light).
- **Fix:** mix it from the palette as the link tint is: a pale amber wash in light, the present brown in dark.
- **Effort:** small. **Recommendation:** Yes.

### 17. Headers are centred over left-aligned columns
- **What:** "Name" is centred above names that start at the left edge, and "Size" is centred above right-aligned numbers. The same in Add links and in Other Accipios.
- **Fix:** each header aligned as its column is.
- **Effort:** small. **Recommendation:** Yes.

### 18. The Rule column is a bare number
- **What:** the Rule column shows "4" (`queue_model.py:163-164`); so does the Add box. The number is the rule's place in the list when the download was added. After you reorder the rules, an old row's "4" points at another rule.
- **Fix:** show the rule's name ("Images"), stored with the row; the number stays in the tooltip and in Why Here?.
- **Effort:** medium. **Recommendation:** Yes.

### 19. A failed operation flashes by in the status bar
- **What:** when something Accipio does fails (not a download: an operation), the last line of the error is shown in the status bar for ten seconds and is then gone (`main_window.py:520-521`). The text is the program's raw error.
- **Fix:** the last problem stays in the status bar until clicked, and a click shows it in full with a Copy button.
- **Effort:** medium. **Recommendation:** Later.

## 4. Rules, Other Accipios, Connect your browser

### 115. Other Accipios shows both roles to everyone
- **What:** a PC usually either sends or collects. The window (`accipio/gui/link_dialog.py`) always shows both: the collecting part (the tick, the address table, the port, the pairing code) fills the top half even on a laptop that only sends, and the two fields a sender needs are below it. The group titles are the only guide ("This PC sends: pair with a collector").
- **A:** two tabs, "Collect here" and "Send from here", with the list of paired Accipios under both.
- **B:** one question at the top, "This PC: sends / collects / both", and only that part is shown.

[[shot:link|Other Accipios today (picture also under 112)]]

[[mock:115]]

**Effort:** medium. **Recommendation:** B (one question in plain words, and the window is half as tall for most people).

### 20. Other Accipios does not say what to do first
- **What:** pairing takes four steps on two PCs in a fixed order (tick an address and accept there; show a code there; paste address and code here; pick the Accipio for a place under Rules). The window explains each control in a tooltip and the order nowhere. Copying an address is a right-click or Ctrl+C, told only in a tooltip.
- **Fix:** three numbered lines at the top of the part in use, and a visible "Copy address" button beside the table, as "Copy Code" already is.
- **Effort:** small. **Recommendation:** Yes.

### 21. The same buttons have different names on the Rules tabs
- **What:** the Rules tab has "+", "Copy", "−" (`rules_dialog.py:879`). Places, File Groups and Site Groups have "+ New" and "Delete".

[[shot:rules-4|The Rules tab: +, Copy, −]]

[[shot:rules-2|File Groups: + New, Delete. In dark mode the page is also a lighter grey than the Rules tab]]

- **Fix:** "+ New", "Copy", "Delete" on all four. And one background for all five tab pages: in dark mode Places, File Groups, Site Groups and Archives are a lighter grey than the Rules tab and the window.
- **Effort:** small. **Recommendation:** Yes.

### 22. Connect your browser has gaps and a stray button
- **What:** the window takes its height before its width is set (`browser_help.py:110`), so the text has large empty bands, and "Copy file" stands above the Firefox paragraph it belongs to.

[[shot:connect|Connect your browser]]

- **Fix:** work out the height after the width; the button level with the first line of its paragraph.
- **Effort:** small. **Recommendation:** Yes.

## Considered and not proposed
- **Add links** holds up: the ticks, the folder, the rule and the status are all on one line, and the button counts ("Add 2"). Its only findings are shared with the main window (cards 17 and 18).
- **Archives on a Rules tab instead of in Settings:** decided on 2026-11-06 (answer 56A); not reopened.
- **The Test box under all five Rules tabs:** it takes room on Places and Archives, but it was built that way on purpose and is useful on each.
- **The numbered tab names ("1 · Places"):** they show the order of setting up; the window opening on tab 4 is deliberate.
- **The build number in the status bar:** it is the only thing always shown there. The speed could move there, but it is fine beside the chips.
- **"Rules…" and "Settings…" as buttons beside the paste bar:** they repeat the Tools menu, and they are the two things a new user looks for.
- **The faint empty track of a progress bar in dark mode:** already on the open list from the last thread (item 5); not counted again here.
